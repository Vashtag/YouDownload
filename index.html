How to use it

  1. Download the app for your computer above.
  2. Open it. On Windows, if you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. On Mac, see below.
  3. Paste a YouTube link, pick Video or MP3, and press Download. Files go to your Downloads folder.

Questions

Why does Windows or my Mac warn me about the app?

The app isn't signed with a paid developer certificate, so your computer doesn't recognise the publisher. On Windows click More info, then Run anyway. On Mac, unzip it, move YouDownload to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open (or allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security). You only need to do this once.

Why is it an app and not a website?

YouTube doesn't allow websites to download videos from your browser. An app on your computer can, without sending anything through someone else's server.

Why is the download big?

It includes everything it needs (the yt-dlp downloader, ffmpeg for video and MP3 conversion, and a JavaScript engine YouTube requires), so there's nothing else to install.
